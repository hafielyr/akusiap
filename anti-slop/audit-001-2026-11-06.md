## Part 3: Landing page upgrade plan (`landing-page-design` output format)

**Design read:** a conversion landing page for Indonesian job seekers and graduates preparing for one selection exam, mostly on mid-range Android phones with limited data. The visual language is your bright blue study-app look with a yellow accent, and the pillar temple is the identity motif. Dials: **ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2**.

**Layout: A (classic hero plus sections).** The product is easy to understand from its own screens, and the traffic is search and social, so a long story page isn't needed.

All copy comes from `docs/unique-selling-points.md` and the existing strings, with no new facts. Items marked **[confirm]** need your yes.

1. **Hero.** Headline (two options, matching the A/B test idea in your USP doc), with no periods and a line break:
   - A: "Fokus ke ujian Anda / CPNS, BUMN, Kedinasan dan UKMPPD"
   - B: "Bayar per ujian / bukan paket besar"

   Subheadline: "Kerjakan 30 soal coba lengkap dengan pembahasan, tanpa daftar. Soal yang salah kembali sebagai kartu hafalan sampai Anda hafal."

   Primary CTA: **"Coba 30 soal gratis"**. The search box moves under the CTA as a small "atau cari soal" link (or stays, see question 2), and "Daftar gratis" leaves the hero.

   Proof line, all facts rather than invented stats: "Tanpa daftar · Pembahasan di setiap soal · Bayar pakai QRIS atau VA".

   The temple art sits beside the text on desktop and is smaller, below the CTA, on phones.
2. **Pick your exam.** The four category tiles, right after the hero, because visitors arrive with one exam in mind.
3. **Problem to solution.** "Bukan pelajaran sekolah. Hanya soal yang keluar di seleksi." Two short lines of text, no cards.
4. **Benefits.** One flagship and three supporting points, with different layouts so they don't read as copy-paste cards:
   - Flagship, full width: **wrong answers come back** (spaced repetition), shown with a real flashcard built from the app's own `.fc` component and labelled "Contoh tampilan".
   - **Try before you pay**: 30 questions with explanations.
   - **Pay per exam**: or subscribe for everything.
   - **Light on your phone**: answers save even when the signal drops. **[confirm]** whether to state the measured "under 150 KB" page budget from OPS-01. It's real and tested.
5. **Tagline reveal** (LPD-B11): "Soal yang salah akan kembali / sampai Anda hafal". Each word fades from 30% to full color as it scrolls in. A small IntersectionObserver script, deferred and about 1 KB. Under reduced motion it shows at full color right away.
6. **How it works.** Your real three steps (Coba gratis, Daftar, Ulangi setiap hari) as a connected timeline, not round numbered icons.
7. **Make your own questions.** One compact row: write questions, share a link, and friends take the exam with just a name.
8. **FAQ.** Native `<details>`, which works with the keyboard and needs no JavaScript. Answers come from the docs:
   - "Apakah benar gratis?"
   - "Perlu daftar akun?"
   - "Bagaimana cara bayar?" **[confirm]** which methods are live: QRIS, VA, e-wallet, card.
   - "Berapa lama akses saya?"
   - "Apakah ini aplikasi resmi BKN?" (No.)
   - "Bisa dipakai dengan sinyal lemah?"
   - "Bagaimana kalau kunci jawaban salah?" (Report it from the review page.)
   - "Bisa buat soal sendiri?"

   Refunds are left out until AC-05 and PAY-09 are settled.
9. **Final CTA.** Identical to the hero: "Coba 30 soal gratis".

**Removed from the landing page:** the fake dashboard showcase (17), the bento (8), "Segera hadir" (27) and the "Baru" badges (26).

**SEO / AEO:** index the page. Title "Latihan soal CPNS, BUMN, Kedinasan dan UKMPPD · LatihanSoal". Add a meta description of about 150 characters, `FAQPage` JSON-LD, and Open Graph tags. The `og:image` needs an asset; per R-23, I'd make it from the logo and temple art only if you say yes.

**Build order:** section by section (hero, benefits, how it works, proof, FAQ, final CTA). The English strings mirror the Indonesian. HomePageTests and ThemeToggleTests are updated with it. I'll measure the first load against the 150 KB budget before and after.

**Notes on conflicting rules:**
- `landing-page-design` bans hyphens in copy, but Indonesian needs them for reduplication (*ragu-ragu*). I'll keep those and avoid all other hyphens.
- `landing-page-design` wants "social proof" above the fold. There is no real proof data yet, so per R-17 and R-36 the proof line holds plain facts and no invented numbers.
