## Landing page as built

The page runs in this order:
- hero
- exam picker
- "Bukan pelajaran sekolah"
- flagship (wrong answers become flashcards)
- three benefits and a list of five more features
- tagline that lights up word by word
- how to start (timeline)
- own questions
- FAQ
- final CTA

Motion is in `wwwroot/js/home.js` (1 KB gzipped). Sections rise once through IntersectionObserver. Tagline words light up in reading order, driven by a scroll listener throttled with requestAnimationFrame, which only listens while the tagline is near the screen. Content stays visible if the script doesn't run, and reduced motion shows everything at once.

## Verified

- I ran the app and checked it at 360×640 (light and dark), 768 and 1024 px wide. There was no horizontal overflow, and every visible control is at least 44 px tall.
- The phone menu opens full height, locks scrolling and closes with Escape. The FAQ opens. All tagline words light up.
- I computed contrast for every new pair (see D-116). Two pairs are icon-only and pass the 3:1 non-text bar: the green check icons at 4.41 and the dark slate-400 icons at 3.33.
- CSS is 10.6 KB gzipped, up from 10.4.
- Tests were updated or added: `HomePageTests` (E2E: first-screen button, FAQ, tagline, phone menu), `ThemeToggleTests` (the toggle in the menu), `LocalizationTests` (new heading), and integration `HomePageTests` (meta, og, JSON-LD).
