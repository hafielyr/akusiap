# LatihanSoal: unique selling points

Possible reasons for a learner to choose LatihanSoal over general learning platforms such as Pahamify and Ruangguru, or over other CPNS/BUMN tryout apps. Each point says whether the app can already back it up.

> **Status key:** ✅ built (merged, or in PR #71 for the rebrand) · 🛠️ planned (card exists) · 💡 idea (no card yet)
>
> Competitor comparisons are **assumptions to verify** before any marketing use. Check each competitor's current app, prices and features, and never name a competitor in ads without legal review.

## Positioning in one sentence

**LatihanSoal is the phone-first practice app for Indonesia's selection exams (CPNS, BUMN, sekolah kedinasan and UKMPPD). Try before you pay, buy only the exam you need, and the app brings back the questions you got wrong until you know them.**

Who it's for: job seekers and graduates preparing for one specific selection exam, often on a mid-range Android phone with limited data. They want to pass one exam, not join a school-curriculum platform.

## Core selling points

### 1. Built for selection exams, not school subjects ✅ (positioning) / 🛠️ (CAT tryouts)
- **What:** one app for CPNS (SKD/SKB), BUMN (TKD and Akhlak), sekolah kedinasan (STAN, IPDN, STIS…) and UKMPPD.
- **Why it matters:** general platforms are mostly organised around school grades and university entrance. Selection-exam learners have to dig for their content.
- **Proof today:** exam categories on the home page, an exam catalog with search, and timed simulations with a pass mark and results per topic.
- **Next:** TRY-01 (a CAT screen like the official system) and TRY-02 (TWK/TIU/TKP scores with a passing grade per subtest).
- *Competitor gap to verify:* how deep Pahamify and Ruangguru go in CPNS/BUMN/UKMPPD content, and whether they offer a CAT-style tryout.

### 2. Try 30 questions free, with full explanations, without signing up ✅
- **What:** every paid exam has a fixed trial set. Guests can do it straight away, and every trial question comes with the explanation and key fact.
- **Why it matters:** the learner judges question quality before paying or even registering.
- **Proof today:** the guest trial (D-08), explanations in trial results, and "Daftar gratis" with progress kept after registering (SHR-04).
- *Competitor gap to verify:* how much of each competitor's content is visible before payment or registration.

### 3. Pay for one exam, not a big bundle ✅ (model) / ⏳ (live payments)
- **What:** buy a single exam for a set number of access days, or subscribe if you want everything. Prices are in Rupiah, paid by QRIS or virtual account.
- **Why it matters:** many learners prepare for one exam once. A low, one-off price per exam fits that better than a long subscription.
- **Proof today:** single-exam purchases and packages, checkout, payment instructions, receipts and expiry reminders.
- **Not live yet:** the DOKU gateway (PAY-04) and the final refund policy.
- *Competitor gap to verify:* whether competitors sell single exam packages or only bundles and subscriptions.

### 4. Wrong answers come back until you know them ✅
- **What:** every question you get wrong becomes a flashcard automatically. Spaced repetition (SM-2) brings it back at the right time, and "Hari ini" shows what's due.
- **Why it matters:** most tryout apps give a score and move on. This app turns each tryout into a personal review plan.
- **Proof today:** wrong answers become review cards (MEM-01), flashcard sets with ratings (MEM-03), scheduled reviews (SRS-02), the daily due list (SRS-03), and "Ulangi yang salah" retakes (SES-09).
- *Competitor gap to verify:* whether any competitor turns tryout mistakes into spaced-repetition cards automatically.

### 5. Light and fast on cheap phones and slow data ✅
- **What:** each page is under 150 KB on first load, with no heavy app download. It is designed at 360 px width, and answers are saved as you go, so a dropped connection doesn't lose progress.
- **Why it matters:** a large share of learners study on entry-level Android phones with limited data.
- **Proof today:** the OPS-01 performance budget, which is tested on every change (catalog 141.5 KB, player 148.5 KB), plus autosave (SES-04).
- *Competitor gap to verify:* the competitors' app download sizes and first-load data use.

### 6. Make your own questions and share them with friends ✅
- **What:** learners write their own questions and exams, practise them, and share a link. Friends can take the exam with just a name; no account is needed.
- **Why it matters:** study groups, mentors and small tutoring groups (bimbel) can use LatihanSoal as a free channel, and every shared link brings new users.
- **Proof today:**
  - own questions and exams (OWN-03/04);
  - share links with reset (SHR-01);
  - guest takers (SHR-03);
  - takedown for reported content (SHR-02);
  - a register invite after a shared result (SHR-04).
- **Next (💡):** let the creator see who took their exam and their scores (story C-09, marked "Later"; not yet a card).

### 7. Mix your own questions with the exams you own ✅
- **What:** a private practice set that combines your own questions with chosen topics from exams you have access to.
- **Why it matters:** learners can focus on their weak topics across sources.
- **Proof today:** the mixed practice set (OWN-06).

### 8. Questions get fixed quickly ✅
- **What:** "Laporkan soal" on every reviewed question, and an admin queue grouped by question.
- **Why it matters:** wrong answer keys are a common complaint about tryout apps. Visible, fast fixes build trust.
- **Proof today:** question reports and the admin queue (SES-11).
- **Marketing angle (💡):** publish "X soal diperbaiki bulan ini" once there is data.

### 9. Daily habit with points and streaks 🛠️
- **What:** a daily 10-question drill, points for correct answers, and a streak counted in Indonesian time (WIB).
- **Why it matters:** consistency beats cramming for selection exams, and streaks keep learners coming back.
- **Status:** cards DRL-01 (drill, points, streak) and DRL-02 (badges and leaderboard; needs a privacy decision).

### 10. Local by default ✅
- **What:** Indonesian first (English available), WIB times, Rupiah prices, QRIS and virtual accounts, and WhatsApp support.
- **Why it matters:** fewer barriers for first-time online learners.
- **Proof today:** localisation, Jakarta time handling, payment channels and the WhatsApp button.

## What not to claim yet
- **"CAT seperti asli" / "mirip sistem BKN":** only after TRY-01 and TRY-02 ship. Even then, avoid implying any official link with BKN or the government.
- **Pass rates, ranking claims, or "soal yang pasti keluar":** no data supports them, and they are risky under consumer-protection rules.
- **Refund promises:** the policy is still pending (PAY-09, AC-05).
- **Prices compared with named competitors:** only with current, verified prices and legal review.
- **Leaderboards with real names:** the privacy decision is still open (DRL-02).
